## Films and posters

The original MP4s were already supplied in the project's `public` directory. The official site links the same films:

- Charter: https://sck01.ams3.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/sckaviation-landing/misc/vid-charter-feature-v1.mp4
- Mission: Impossible: https://sck01.ams3.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/sckaviation-landing/misc/vid-mi-opt-v1.mp4

`public/videos/charter.mp4` is a 720 × 1280 H.264 copy of the original 2160 × 3840 charter film. `public/videos/atelier.mp4` is a 1920 × 1012 H.264 copy of the original 4096 × 2160 production film. Both preserve the whole frame, use fast-start metadata and retain audio. No image or video is enlarged or cropped by the playback component.

`public/images/charter-poster.webp` is a frame from the charter film at 2.5 seconds. `public/images/atelier-poster.webp` is a frame from the production film at 5 seconds. Both were visually checked against the associated film.
